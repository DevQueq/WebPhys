What the model is

Each lattice site carries one displacement (a single polarisation, like one shear component) with unit mass. Neighbours are joined by springs with force k·s + βs³, where s is the stretch. Boundaries wrap around. A symplectic (velocity Verlet) integrator conserves energy apart from a small bounded wobble, shown as the total energy change.

How coherence is measured

Each member is a standing wave cos(k·x) with |k| fixed at 5 lattice units: directions (3,4), (4,3), (3,−4), (4,−3), then (5,0) and (0,5). Projecting the field onto each gives a complex amplitude, so its phase is exact at every instant. r is the Kuramoto order parameter of those phases.

What it leaves out

There is no solvent phase, so there are no fast or slow compressional branches: this is the network's shear branch alone. Lattice anisotropy makes members 5 and 6 slightly off frequency. "Energy still in the set" uses linear mode energies, so with strong nonlinearity it can read above 100% at first.
